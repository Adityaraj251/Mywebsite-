GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Some helpful details before we start talking about your project.

Have another question? Contact me ↗
How much does a website cost?+

Pricing depends on the number of pages, design requirements, content, and features. Share your idea and budget, and we can discuss a practical scope before agreeing on a price.

How long will my website take?+

Timing depends on the project scope, content readiness, feedback, and features. I can give a more useful estimate after understanding your requirements.

Will my website work on mobile?+

Responsive behavior is part of the design approach, with layouts planned for phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

Do you provide domain and hosting?+

Domain registration and hosting needs can be discussed as part of planning. Their availability, cost, and setup depend on the provider and agreed project scope.

Can I request changes after seeing the design?+

Yes, the review process and included revision scope will be discussed and agreed before the project begins. Additional work may affect the final cost or timeline.

How do I get started?+

Send a message on WhatsApp or use the inquiry form. Tell me what your business does, what type of website you need, and any preferences you already have.
